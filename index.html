MODULE 01 · READY TO LEARN

What is Power BI?

Understand the purpose before learning the buttons.

CHAPTER 01 · ABOUT 10 MINUTESWhat you will learn

Why businesses use Power BI, how it works, and how a business question becomes a report.

1. Start with the business problem

Imagine a retail company with three stores. Every month, the manager receives separate Excel files for sales and sales targets. The manager wants to know: Are we meeting our targets, and which store needs attention?

Looking at thousands of rows takes time. A Power BI report can combine the data, calculate the results and make it easier to see the answer.

2. What is Power BI?

Power BI is Microsoft's business intelligence and reporting platform. It helps you connect to data, prepare it, create relationships and calculations, and build interactive reports for business decisions.

Simple definition to remember
Power BI turns business data into useful information that people can explore and act on.
3. Understand the main components
ComponentWhat it does
Power BI DesktopBuild reports on your computer.
Power QueryClean and transform data before analysis.
Semantic modelOrganise tables, relationships and calculations.
DAXCreate calculations such as Total Sales and Sales Variance.
Power BI ServicePublish, view and manage reports online. Sharing permissions depend on licensing and setup.
4. See a real business example

The example below uses small, made-up figures in ₹ lakh. Variance means Actual Sales minus Sales Target.

StoreActualTargetVariance
Malleshwaram2.53.0−0.5
Rajajinagar4.24.0+0.2
Jayanagar3.83.5+0.3
Total10.510.50.0
Business insight

The company met its overall target, but Malleshwaram missed its target by ₹0.5 lakh. The manager should investigate that store instead of assuming every store performed well.

5. Which visuals would help?
KPI cardsShow total actual sales, target and variance at a glance.
Clustered column chartCompare actual sales and target for each store.
TableShow exact figures so managers can verify the differences.
SlicerLet managers filter by store or month when the report includes those fields.
6. Desktop vs Service

Power BI Desktop: where an analyst typically prepares the model and builds the report. Power BI Service: where teams publish, access and manage content online.

Helpful distinction
A report can contain multiple pages and interactive visuals. A Power BI dashboard is a single-page canvas created in the Power BI Service using pinned tiles.
7. Five things to remember
  1. Start with the business question, not the chart.
  2. Use Power Query to prepare the data.
  3. Use the semantic model and DAX for analysis.
  4. Pick visuals that make comparisons easy.
  5. Validate your numbers before sharing a report.
Try it yourself (no software needed)

For a school with student marks and attendance, write down two useful KPIs, one chart, and one decision the principal could make from the report.
